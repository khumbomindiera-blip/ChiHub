## Brand & Style
The design system reflects an energetic, nurturing, and trustworthy sanctuary for creative growth in Blantyre, Malawi. The personality seamlessly bridges childhood wonder with parental reassurance: curious, sunny, grounded, and structurally dependable.

Drawing from modern tactile warmth and soft neo-digital playfulness, the visual language avoids sterile corporate SaaS patterns and chaotic kindergarten tropes alike. Instead, it balances high-contrast, rounded typography with organic pill geometry, sun-drenched accents, and lilac-bathed neutral canvases. It communicates safety and institutional stability to parents, while sparking imagination and joyful exploration in young learners.

## Colors
The palette is built around an anchor of deep, royal purples paired with energetic sunshine ambers and fresh meadow greens:

- **Primary (`#5B21B6`, `#7C3AED`, `#4C1D95`)**: Represents dignity, depth, and focused imagination. The deep violet provides anchor-weight for primary CTAs and critical structural elements, reassuring parents with steady presence.
- **Secondary (`#F59E0B`, `#FBBF24`)**: Sunshine Gold and Amber invoke Malawian warmth, outdoor joy, and high-energy encouragement. Used for active badges, celebration states, and focal highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981`)**: Fresh Meadow Green introduces an organic, lively touch point—signifying growth, nature, correct answers, and physical activity milestones.
- **Canvas & Neutrals (`#FAF7FD`, `#F3EFFB`, `#FFFFFF`)**: Tinted lilac-slate backgrounds create a serene, clean stage that reduces screen glare while allowing white cards to pop naturally without harsh edge borders.
- **Ink & Typography (`#1E1B4B`, `#332B50`)**: Deep saturated midnight slate provides WCAG AAA contrast against lilac canvases, maintaining soft warmth without the harshness of pitch black.

## Typography
Plus Jakarta Sans delivers geometric precision with rounded terminals, instilling an approachable, cheerful cadence across all touchpoints while maintaining high legibility for young readers and parents.

- **Headlines & Display:** Heavy weights (700 and 800) create expressive, bubbly title cards that command attention without looking chaotic. Tight letter-spacing maintains structural cohesion.
- **Body:** Standard weights (400 and 500) paired with generous line heights (1.5x - 1.6x) ensure long-form instructional guides, activity summaries, and parent reports remain effortless to read.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** High-weight, slightly tracked tokens give chips, interactive toggles, and quiz indicators immediate readability at small scales.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a fluid-responsive 12-column grid system for desktop and tablet screens, folding down to a dedicated single-column format on mobile devices.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, 1.5rem (24px) gutters, and 2rem (32px) margins. Cards span contextual 3-, 4-, 6-, or 12-column blocks with generous internal breathing room to preserve a playful, uncluttered canvas.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, 1.25rem (20px) gutters, and 1.5rem (24px) margins.
- **Mobile (below 768px):** 4-column responsive grid with 1rem (16px) gutters and 1.25rem (20px) margins. Cards stack vertically, maintaining minimum 44px touch target zones throughout all actionable components.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows comfortable increments, prioritizing spacious layouts (`space-lg`, `space-xl`) around visual illustrations and interactive widgets to avoid overwhelming young users.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation relies on ambient, tinted shadows rather than dark, neutral dropshadows, reinforcing a luminous and welcoming physical space:

- **Base Level (Canvas):** Flat lilac-slate surface (`#FAF7FD`).
- **Surface Level 1 (Cards & Modules):** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with an ambient diffused glow: `0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(91, 33, 182, 0.07), 0px 2px 6px -1px rgba(91, 33, 182, 0.04)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Interactive Floating Elements / Popovers):** Elevated cards, menus, and the floating chat bubble: `0px 16px 36px -6px rgba(91, 33, 182, 0.12), 0px 4px 12px -2px rgba(91, 33, 182, 0.06)`.
- **Active / Pressed State:** Elements depress downward with reduced Y-offset and tighter blur: `0px 4px 12px -2px rgba(91, 33, 182, 0.08)`.
- **Outlines & Borders:** Soft micro-borders use lavender tints (`#EDE9FE` at 1.5px) rather than harsh greys to preserve a gentle contour.

## Shapes
A pill-shaped, ultra-friendly curvature characterizes all interactive and structural containers:

- **Cards & Gauges:** Range between 20px (`rounded-lg`) and 28px (`rounded-xl`) corner radii, generating soft, pillow-like modular pods that feel safe and physically tangible.
- **Buttons, Badges & Chips:** Fully rounded continuous pill shapes (`border-radius: 9999px`) provide an inviting, thumb-friendly surface that encourages direct interaction.
- **Input Fields & Search Bars:** Generously curved at 16px to 24px, ensuring inputs visually harmonize with the pill buttons and round cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Pill:** Background in Deep Purple (`#5B21B6`), label in white (`#FFFFFF`), full pill radius (9999px). Subtly elevated with purple ambient shadow. Hover state brightens to Royal Violet (`#7C3AED`); active state mimics a physical bounce press.
- **Secondary Sunshine Pill:** Warm Yellow (`#FBBF24`) fill with Deep Slate text (`#1E1B4B`). Used for engagement triggers, rewards, and play invitations.
- **Ghost / Soft Pill:** Background in soft lavender tint (`#EDE9FE`), text in `#5B21B6`. Borderless and gentle for secondary workflows.

### Chips & Badges
- **Pill Badges:** 28px to 32px height, full pill radius. Saturated icon container coupled with light tinted backgrounds (e.g., `#FEF3C7` background with `#D97706` text for discovery tiers, `#D1FAE5` with `#059669` for outdoor badges).

### Cards
- **Content & Challenge Pods:** Pure white surface (`#FFFFFF`) with a 24px border-radius, bounded by a 1.5px subtle border (`#EDE9FE`). Padded with `space-lg`. Features an optional pastel accent band (amber, purple, or meadow green) running along the top edge or wrapping title chips.

### Interactive Quiz Score Gauge Card
- Dedicated component with an inner radial circular progress tracker or segmented arch gauge in vibrant gold (`#F59E0B`) and meadow green (`#10B981`). Encased in a 28px rounded white card with an upbeat headline, energetic feedback pill badge (e.g., "Super Explorer!"), and immediate replay/next-step actions.

### Floating Chat Widget
- **Launcher Button:** 60px circle or squircle-pill in Royal Violet (`#7C3AED`) with a gentle pulsing ambient shadow and friendly mascot or chat icon.
- **Chat Window Pod:** 28px radius card floating 24px from screen bounds. Uses a soft lilac header with a meadow green online beacon dot, rounded speech bubbles (18px radius) with deep slate legible text, and a rounded pill input dock.

### Form Inputs
- 16px border-radius fields with `#F5F3FF` background and `#EDE9FE` border. On focus, transitions cleanly to a 2px royal violet outline with a 4px soft purple glow (`rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.15)`). Deep slate text (`#1E1B4B`) with friendly, descriptive placeholder text.

### Checkboxes & Radio Selectors
- Large, kid-friendly 24px hit targets with 8px rounded corners for checkboxes and fully circular shapes for radio buttons. Deep purple fill with crisp white checkmarks on selection.
